Florida's 25th District is an unusual South Florida contest: a Democratic incumbent seeking reelection in a newly drawn district that Trump carried in 2024. Jared Moskowitz, who now represents FL-23, faces Scott Singer, the former mayor of Boca Raton. Libertarian Peter Jassenoff is on the ballot, plus certified write-in Michaelangelo Hamilton.

The story is Florida's 2026 redistricting. Moskowitz isn't defending the district he won in 2024; he's running in a substantially reassembled constituency with a different partisan profile.

| Scott Singer | Republican | Attorney, business owner, Boca Raton mayor until 2026; endorsed by Trump and DeSantis |

## The district

Florida adopted a new map in May 2026 that rearranged districts across Broward and Palm Beach counties. The new FL-25 has no clear predecessor. Approximate population shares from old districts:

| Predecessor | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Former FL-23 | 46% |
| Former FL-24 | 30% |
| Former FL-25 | 16% |
| Other districts | 8% |

So Moskowitz knew much of the area, but most residents are new to him. The redistricting set off a chain of moves: Democrat Debbie Wasserman Schultz, who held the old FL-25, is running in the new FL-20, and Moskowitz moved from FL-23 to FL-25.

## Candidates

### Jared Moskowitz (D)

Moskowitz, 45, was first elected in 2022 after serving in the Florida House and being appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis as director of the state's Division of Emergency Management, including during COVID. In Congress he has cultivated bipartisan engagement and is outspoken on Israel and antisemitism. His campaign stresses affordability, emergency preparedness, public safety and healthcare. He won the August 18 primary over Oliver Adams Larkin with 63.5%.

### Scott Singer (R)

An attorney, business owner and former Boca Raton mayor, Singer emphasizes federal spending restraint, tax cuts, public safety, immigration enforcement and the military, and points to his municipal budget and development experience. He won a five-candidate primary with 31.4% over George Moraitis, Dan Franzese, Joe Kaufman and Raven Harrison, with endorsements from Trump and DeSantis.

### Others

Libertarian Peter Jassenoff is on the printed ballot and Michaelangelo Hamilton is a write-in. Neither has drawn comparable coverage or resources.

CBS Miami's October 14 coverage said it could be among the country's most expensive House races, though the national ranking depends on the date and whether candidate and outside money are combined.

## The issues

- **Affordability and spending.** Moskowitz focuses on groceries, insurance and healthcare costs; Singer on cutting federal spending and taxes. Both told CBS Miami affordability is voters' central concern.
- **Immigration.** South Florida's immigrant communities (varied Latin American and Caribbean backgrounds) make federal policy salient. Singer stresses enforcement and border security; Moskowitz criticizes parts of the Trump administration's approach while backing system reforms.
- **Israel and antisemitism.** Both back Israel, so the difference is emphasis and alliances. Moskowitz is a prominent pro-Israel Democrat; Singer stresses his own support and criticizes Moskowitz's alignment with Democratic leadership. Congregation B'nai Israel in Boca Raton scheduled an October 20 candidate forum on the topic.
- **Abortion.** A clearer contrast, per the Guides.vote comparison: Moskowitz supports federal protections for abortion access and has sponsored legislation on state restrictions; Singer supports legal protection for fetuses from conception, with exceptions for rape, incest and threats to the mother's life.
- **Climate, hurricanes, insurance.** Preparedness, flood insurance, sea-level rise and property-insurance costs. Moskowitz's emergency-management background is directly relevant; Singer's is local infrastructure, development and services.

## Why it matters

- **Florida's new map.** Only 46% of the district's population came from Moskowitz's old seat.
- **Crossover voting.** A Democratic incumbent with an established South Florida profile in a district Trump carried by about 9.
- **Executive experience on both sides.** Moskowitz ran statewide emergency management; Singer was mayor of Boca Raton.
- **South Florida realignment.** Alongside [FL-14](../fl-14/), [FL-22](../fl-22/) and [FL-27](../fl-27/), FL-25 is part of a set of competitive races involving changed boundaries, suburban voting, immigration and the region's political direction.

## What to watch

A new independent poll, final independent-expenditure disclosures, and late-October appearances. The October 20 Boca Raton forum, a moderated forum rather than a debate, may clarify differences on Israel, federal spending and national alliances.

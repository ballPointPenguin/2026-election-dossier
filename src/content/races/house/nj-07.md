New Jersey's 7th District is a Republican-held seat in the affluent, politically mixed suburbs and exurbs west of New York City. It has changed parties twice since 2018, and its presidential electorate has moved from narrowly Democratic to narrowly Republican.

The November 3 race pits Republican incumbent Tom Kean Jr. against Democrat Rebecca Bennett, a Navy veteran and former helicopter pilot. Seamus Patrick O'Toole, under the ballot designation "Stop Israel's Genocide", is the third candidate. It contrasts Kean's long political career with Bennett's military background.

## The district

NJ-7 includes all of Hunterdon and Warren counties and parts of Morris, Somerset, Sussex and Union counties: Westfield, Bridgewater, Flemington, Clinton and rural northwestern towns.

| Area | Character |
| --- | --- |
| Union County portion, incl. Westfield | Affluent commuter suburbs, professionals |
| Somerset County, incl. Bridgewater | Pharmaceuticals, healthcare, corporate offices, suburbs |
| Hunterdon County | Affluent suburbs and exurbs, agriculture, historic towns, open space |
| Warren County | Small cities, rural communities, manufacturing, logistics |
| Morris and Sussex portions | Suburbs and exurbs, professionals, small business, farming |

It mixes households tied to New York's economy with communities where farming, manufacturing and local services matter more, so property taxes, income taxes, housing costs and commuting sit beside rural infrastructure and conservation.

## Candidates

### Tom Kean Jr. (R)

Kean served in the General Assembly and state Senate, including 14 years as Senate Republican leader, before winning in 2022, and is seeking a third term. On Energy and Commerce and Foreign Affairs, he emphasizes taxes, energy prices, public safety, healthcare affordability and federal resources for the district. He has generally supported the Trump administration's priorities while presenting himself as an independent advocate for New Jersey.

### Rebecca Bennett (D)

She worked in healthcare including at Johnson & Johnson and in digital-health businesses and has never held elected office. Her campaign stresses affordability, healthcare access, accountability and congressional oversight of the Trump administration. She won the June 2 primary:

She's part of a 2026 pattern of Democrats nominating veterans and national-security professionals, with a combination of military and healthcare experience unlike William Lawrence in [MI-7](../mi-07/) or Randy Villegas in [CA-22](../ca-22/).

## Kean's absence from Congress

Kean was absent from in-person House proceedings for about four months in 2026, missing more than 100 votes. He returned in June and said he'd been hospitalized for treatment of depression. His initial silence drew scrutiny over representation and transparency, and Bennett has raised accountability and constituent-representation questions. Kean calls the matter private and says he's focused on recovery and his duties. The documented facts are the absence and missed votes; they say nothing about his present health or ability to serve, and medical treatment isn't evidence of misconduct.

## The September 27 debate

Their only scheduled debate, sponsored by the New Jersey Globe and Rider University, covered Iran, tariffs, healthcare, immigration, the 2025 tax law and congressional accountability.

| Issue | Kean (R) | Bennett (D) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tariffs | Defended Trump's authority to impose them | Congress should restrict unilateral presidential tariff power |
| Iran war | Supported force to stop Iran getting nuclear weapons | Opposed continuing the war; linked it to fuel costs |
| Healthcare | Affordability, market reforms, his legislative work | Coverage, costs, her sector experience |
| Taxes | Supported the 2025 law | Criticized its distribution and healthcare changes |
| Oversight | Willing to challenge federal decisions hurting New Jersey | Congress hasn't exercised adequate oversight |

**Iran.** Both have national-security backgrounds. Kean defended the use of force, citing Iran's nuclear ambitions; Bennett, citing her Navy service, said it should end and Congress should control continuing operations, a disagreement about foreign policy and about war powers.

**Immigration.** An ICE detention facility in the district makes federal immigration policy local. They differed on enforcement authority, legal safeguards and accountability.

## Why it matters nationally

- **Two party changes since 2018.** Lance lost to Malinowski in 2018, who lost to Kean in 2022.
- **A narrow presidential electorate.** Trump won by about a point though Democrats have won other recent races here.
- **A long-career Republican.** Kean's family and legislative history differ from newer Republicans like Gabe Evans in [CO-8](../co-08/) and Tom Barrett in MI-7.
- **A new kind of challenger.** Military and healthcare experience against a longtime legislator.
- **An expensive media market.** One of the country's costliest.
- **Suburban voting.** Wealthy commuter towns, exurbs and rural areas differ from industrial battlegrounds like [PA-7](../pa-07/) and MI-7, with household costs, healthcare and federal economic policy still central.
